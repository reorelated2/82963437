# Requirements

Kleinman Desk is the working desk for Kyle Kleinman, a Redfin agent in Miami Dade and Broward. Redfin Partner Tools stays the system of record. This desk does not replace it and does not write back to it.

## First release

New lead or screenshot, then:

1. Read the facts that are actually present.
2. Match an existing contact, or hold an uncertain match for review, or create one new contact.
3. Prepare a buyer summary.
4. Draft the next message in Kyle's voice.
5. Suggest the next action and a follow up time.
6. Prepare a CRM note Kyle can paste into Redfin.
7. Put all of that in a review queue.

Nothing is texted, emailed, or posted from the queue.

## Facts

When present, capture name, phone, email, lead source, assigned agent, property address or MLS number, requested showing time, available time, confirmed showing time, preferred areas, budget, home use, financing, timeline, motivation, must haves, deal breakers, previous communication, and a stated next action.

Missing facts stay `Data needed`. Unclear text stays unclear. Conflicting values are kept side by side. The original value is not overwritten.

Do not infer financing approval, a confirmed appointment, or consent from incomplete evidence.

## Daily question

The opening screen answers: who needs attention, why, and what to do next.

It shows new leads, conversations that need a reply, today's appointments, overdue follow ups, upcoming milestones, drafts, and failed automations.

## Voice

Short, natural, confident, warm, and direct. One purpose. Usually one question. No corporate language. No dashes in client copy. Amounts look like `$650K`. Do not invent familiarity, urgency, competing offers, or property facts.

## Out of scope until a later module

Contact import and reactivation, buyer qualification beyond this summary, property matching, CMA, investor analysis, offers, marketing publication, and performance reporting. Those stay on the backlog.
