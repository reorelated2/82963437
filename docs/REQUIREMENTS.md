# Requirements

Kyle Kleinman, Redfin agent, Miami-Dade and Broward. The desk exists to cut daily busywork. Redfin stays the brokerage system of record. This desk holds working copies, drafts, and reminders. It does not write to Redfin and it does not send messages.

## Priorities

1. Turn a new lead into a conversation and a next step.
2. Keep accurate notes and consistent follow ups.
3. Qualify buyers without extra friction.
4. Find and judge properties.
5. Win seller appointments and price listings from evidence.
6. Track offers, negotiations, and milestones.
7. Reactivate eligible past leads.
8. See what produces appointments, contracts, and income.

## First release

New lead or screenshot, then:

1. Extract facts that are actually present.
2. Match a contact by phone or email, or open a new record for review.
3. Prepare a buyer summary.
4. Draft the next text in Kyle's voice.
5. Suggest the next action and a follow up time.
6. Prepare a CRM note to paste into Redfin.
7. Put the package in the review queue.

Missing facts stay "Data needed." Conflicting facts are shown and do not overwrite the saved record. A requested showing is not called confirmed. Financing is recorded only when the source states pre-approved, pre-qualified, cash, or needs a lender.

## Voice

Short, natural, confident, warm, direct. One purpose. Usually one question. No canned enthusiasm. No greeting when the conversation is already going. Money is written `$650K`. Client texts do not use dashes as punctuation. The desk does not invent familiarity, urgency, competing offers, or property facts.

## Daily screen

The opening screen answers who needs attention, why, and what to do next. It lists failed automations, overdue follow ups, new leads, replies, drafts, today, and upcoming milestones.

## Every feature

Each feature needs a business purpose, inputs, outputs, a data source, a trigger, visible success and failure, and a check that it works. An empty screen is not a finished feature.

## Not in this release

Contact import, buyer tours, MLS matching, CMA, investor math, offers, marketing, and performance stats are backlog items. They are not built yet.
